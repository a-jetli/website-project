# Pages are fine to crawl. The portrait is not: keep it out of image search.
#
# Do NOT add a blanket image rule here. favicon.png has to stay crawlable or
# Google Search drops back to a generic globe icon next to the result.
#
# No "Allow: /" line here on purpose. Crawling is permitted by default, and an
# explicit Allow makes the rule ambiguous: Google resolves conflicts by longest
# match, older parsers by first match, so they would disagree about the photo.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /header_photo.jpg
